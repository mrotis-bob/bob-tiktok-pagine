Privacy Policy

App: Bob (personal desktop assistant) · Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Who uses this app

Bob is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on a private Windows computer. Its only TikTok feature is uploading the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account with the official TikTok APIs.

2. Data the app receives from TikTok

  • Access token and refresh token, given by TikTok after you authorize the app. They are needed to upload videos and are renewed automatically.
  • Basic account information (open ID and display name) and the posting options TikTok allows for the account (for example the available privacy levels), only to show which account is connected and to post correctly.
  • The upload result (publish ID and status) of the videos you send.

The app does not read your followers, messages, contacts, likes, location or other people's data.

3. Where the data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's computer. The tokens are saved in a local file on that PC only. There is no server, no cloud database and no analytics. The app sends data only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to upload the videos you choose.

4. Sharing and selling

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. No advertising, no tracking, no profiling.

5. Keeping and deleting data

The tokens are kept until you disconnect. To delete everything: remove the app's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the token file on the computer. TikTok's own handling of your data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The app is not meant for children and is not offered to anyone other than its owner.
