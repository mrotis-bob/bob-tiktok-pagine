App: Bob (personal desktop assistant) · Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. What this app is

Bob is a personal assistant that runs on one private Windows computer. It is used by its owner only, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok APIs (Login Kit and Content Posting API). It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no other users, and nothing is sold.

2. What the app does on TikTok

  • It asks you to log in with TikTok and to authorize it (permissions: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • It reads your basic profile (display name) to show which account is connected.
  • It uploads videos you chose either as drafts to your TikTok inbox, or as direct posts with the privacy setting allowed by TikTok. Videos made with AI are always marked as AI-generated content.
  • It never posts anything you did not choose, never likes, follows, comments or sends messages, and never accesses other people's accounts.

3. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the videos you upload and must follow the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Only upload content you own or have the right to use.

4. Disconnecting

You can disconnect the app at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or by deleting the token file on the computer. After that the app can no longer access your account.
